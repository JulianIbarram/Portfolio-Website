Knight Light Web Developer

Designed and developed a professional website for Knight Light to showcase drone show services and attract potential clients.

Designed the panorama system to give clients and team members a more realistic viewing angle, significantly improving how drone formations, spacing, and visual effects could be evaluated before live deployment.

Streamlined the selection process for 50+ prebuilt drone show presets by enabling customers to preview simulations directly inside the virtual stadium environment and quickly choose their preferred show concepts.

Reduced development and revision time by eliminating the need for repeated manual adjustments, saving the project hours of testing and helping the team move faster from concept to client approval.

Created the panoramic stadium model in Blender, optimizing it for web performance before integrating the exported asset into the JavaScript-based simulation platform.

Connected 3D assets with front-end functionality to ensure smooth rendering, responsive controls, and seamless interaction between the panoramic environment and drone show presets.

Combined skills in web development, 3D modeling, API integration, user experience design, and client-focused problem solving to deliver a polished and practical simulation tool.
