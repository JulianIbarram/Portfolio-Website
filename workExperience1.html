Horse Plinko Plinktern

A Plinktern in the Horse Plinko Cyber Security Challenge is typically a participant who solves cybersecurity puzzles and hands-on challenges involving areas like cryptography, networking, web security, and problem-solving. The role focuses on learning practical security skills, thinking critically under pressure, and collaborating to complete challenges efficiently.

Defended a network of 8 critical devices during a live cybersecurity competition while systems were under continuous attack from multiple unidentified threat actors attempting to deploy malicious scripts, steal sensitive information, delete data, and disrupt network operations.

Monitored compromised systems in real time, identified suspicious activity, and applied defensive measures to contain threats, restore stability, and maintain functionality across the network under pressure.

Collaborated closely with a 4-person incident response team to detect, isolate, and remove 50+ malicious scripts, allowing key servers and services to come back online faster and minimizing downtime.

Assisted in prioritizing threats based on severity and impact, ensuring the most critical systems were secured first while maintaining efficient communication with teammates throughout the event.

Investigated network logs and system activity to uncover 5 hidden forensic puzzles, analyzing clues and identifying concealed messages embedded within the data.

Compiled findings into a clear technical report and presented results to judges after the defense stage, demonstrating analytical ability, technical communication skills, and contributing to a higher overall team score.

Strengthened hands-on skills in network defense, malware detection, log analysis, teamwork, incident response, and problem-solving in high-pressure environments.
